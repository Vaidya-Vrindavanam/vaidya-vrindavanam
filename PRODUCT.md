# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Two primary audiences. First: local Kerala patients in Haripad, Alappuzha, and surrounding districts — often middle-aged, dealing with chronic pain, lifestyle diseases, or seeking seasonal Panchakarma. They arrive by referral or by searching for a nearby Ayurveda hospital. Second: NRIs and medical tourists who want authentic Kerala Ayurveda for a residential stay — not spa Ayurveda, but the real clinical kind supervised by qualified physicians. Both audiences make a considered booking decision; this is not an impulse purchase. The conversion action is a WhatsApp message or phone call.

## Product Purpose

Drive consultation bookings and residential package enquiries for a 12-year-old Ayurvedic hospital in Haripad, Kerala. The hospital's USP is the combination of classical Marma Chikitsa with modern chiropractic care, operated by MD-qualified physicians (BAMS, MD Ayurveda) who take an unhurried, root-cause approach. Success means a prospective patient trusts the clinic enough to reach out via WhatsApp.

## Brand Personality

Ancient and grounded. Unhurried. Genuinely clinical, not spa-commercial.

Three words: rooted, thorough, serene.

Emotional goal: a patient should feel that this clinic will actually listen and take them seriously — not give them a package deal or rush them through. The design should feel like the hospital itself: a garden in Kerala, calm, permanent, unhurried.

## Anti-references

- Modern Ayurveda spa brands that use warm gold + cream with stock photos of smiling women in robes (Kama Ayurveda, Forest Essentials visual tone) — too commercial, too light.
- Generic dark "premium wellness" sites that look like a gym or retreat centre — not clinical.
- Overly animated, scroll-jacking Indian healthcare sites.
- Any site that looks like it was built in a hurry for SEO clicks.

## Design Principles

1. **Permanence over trend.** The clinic has been here for 12 years and treats serious conditions. The design should feel like it will still be appropriate in 10 years — nothing that dates by next quarter.
2. **Trust through restraint.** Clinical trust is built by what you don't do as much as by what you do. No gratuitous decoration, no hero-metric templates, no identical card grids.
3. **Copy leads, design supports.** The writing on this site is unusually strong (story of the clinic, philosophy section). The design's job is to give that writing space to land, not to compete with it.
4. **Conversion without pressure.** The primary CTA (WhatsApp) should be ever-present and easy to find, but never aggressive. A patient booking an Ayurveda treatment is making a considered choice.
5. **Kerala's materiality.** The dark forest green + antique gold palette is already committed. It evokes Kerala's landscape — paddy fields at dusk, temple gold — not a generic "dark premium" SaaS aesthetic. Every design decision should reinforce that specific geography and culture.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. All text must meet 4.5:1 contrast (AA) against its background. Interactive elements need 44×44px minimum touch targets. Keyboard navigation must be fully functional including a proper focus trap on the mobile navigation overlay. Reduced motion must be respected for the nav-live-dot pulse animation (already implemented).
